Actual landing coordinates (all three landed missions)
NASA NSSDCA Table of Anthropogenic Impacts and Spacecraft on the Moon:
https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/lunar/lunar_artifact_impacts.html
Odysseus IM-1: -80.13 latitude, +1.44 longitude; landed 22 February 2024.
IM-2: -84.7906 latitude, +29.1957 longitude; landed 6 March 2025.
Blue Ghost 1: +18.562 latitude, +61.810 longitude; landed 2 March 2025.
The table links these location entries to LROC observations. Preserve its
precision; it does not specify a full geodetic frame/uncertainty statement.
Do not confuse the IM-1 target -80.297,+1.2613 in preflight NASA material with
the landed location. The IM-2 PDS record gives a rounded -84.79,+29.20 location.
